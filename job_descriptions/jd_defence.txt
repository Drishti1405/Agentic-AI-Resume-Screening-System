Hiring a Defence Cybersecurity Officer to protect mission-critical networks, lead SIEM monitoring, and conduct vulnerability assessment.

- Threat Intelligence & Vulnerability Assessment

Certifications:

Educational Requirements:
- B.S. in Cybersecurity, Computer Science, or Information Assurance

Responsibilities:
- Conduct real-time threat hunting and incident response across classified defence networks.
